Cross-Study Generalization in Immune-Response Prediction: CMI-Flu Challenge

Jul 2026 – Sep 2026

  • Developed cross-study models for the CMI-Flu 1st Prediction Challenge, predicting post-vaccination immune responses from pre-vaccination profiles across 59 studies, 4,805 participant records, and 8 assay modalities (~89M RNA-seq rows).
  • Built cross-study preprocessing and validation pipelines with within-study percentile-rank normalization, modality-specific MICE/kNN imputation, and leave-one-study-out evaluation that excluded repeat participants from training.
  • Designed a two-stage prediction procedure aligned with the challenge evaluation: modeled within-study response percentiles for Spearman-based assessment, then constructed a cohort-specific target response distribution and converted predicted percentiles to absolute response values through quantile matching; validated the procedure with leave-one-study-out and bootstrap analyses.

Family-Based Analysis of Genetic and Socioeconomic Variation in Autism

Apr 2026 – Jun 2026

  • Developed family-aware statistical-genomics workflows for 113,045 individuals from 52,333 SSC and SPARK families, integrating common polygenic risk, rare inherited/de novo variation, measured air pollution, and neighborhood deprivation.
